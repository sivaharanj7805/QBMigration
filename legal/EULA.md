### 4.1 Authorization

- Access your QuickBooks Desktop data for extraction
- Connect to your QuickBooks Online account via OAuth
- Process and transform your data for migration
- Generate cryptographic hashes for verification

### 4.2 Data Handling

- Data is processed in memory and not permanently stored on our servers
