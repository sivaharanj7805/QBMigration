FORENSICBRIDGE

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: January 1, 2026 | Version 1.0

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Account Information

When you create an account, we collect:

  • Full name
  • Email address
  • Company/firm name
  • Password (stored as cryptographic hash only)

1.2 Migration Data

🔒 Zero-Persistence Architecture: Your QuickBooks financial data is encrypted during processing and automatically deleted upon migration completion. We do NOT store your raw financial data.

1.3 What We Retain

Data Type Purpose Retention
Migration metadata Audit trail, billing 7 years
SHA-256 hashes Forensic verification 7 years
Account information Authentication, support Account lifetime
Raw financial data Migration processing NOT STORED

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Provide and operate the ForensicBridge migration service
  • Process your migrations securely
  • Generate audit certificates and compliance reports
  • Communicate with you about your account and migrations
  • Improve our services and develop new features
  • Comply with legal obligations

3. Data Security

3.1 Encryption Standards

Protection Layer Technology
Data at Rest AES-256-GCM
Data in Transit TLS 1.3
Password Storage Argon2id
Forensic Hashing SHA-256

3.2 Data Residency

All data is processed and stored in Canadian data centers (AWS ca-central-1, Montreal) to comply with Canadian privacy regulations including PIPEDA.

3.3 Security Measures

  • Isolated processing environments for each migration
  • Automatic secure deletion after processing
  • Account lockout after 5 failed login attempts
  • Rate limiting on all API endpoints
  • Regular security audits and penetration testing

4. Information Sharing

We do NOT sell your personal information. We may share information only:

  • With Intuit: To authenticate your QuickBooks Online connection via OAuth
  • Service Providers: AWS for hosting, with strict data processing agreements
  • Legal Requirements: When required by law or to protect our rights

5. Your Rights

Under Canadian privacy law (PIPEDA) and GDPR (if applicable), you have the right to:

  • Access: Request a copy of your personal data
  • Rectification: Correct inaccurate personal data
  • Erasure: Request deletion of your account and data
  • Portability: Receive your data in a portable format
  • Withdraw Consent: Disconnect QuickBooks at any time

To exercise these rights, contact privacy@forensicbridge.ca

6. QuickBooks Integration

When you connect ForensicBridge to QuickBooks Online:

  • We use Intuit's OAuth 2.0 for secure authentication
  • You can disconnect at any time via your dashboard or the /disconnect page
  • Upon disconnection, we revoke our access tokens immediately

7. Cookies and Tracking

We use essential cookies only for:

  • Session management and authentication
  • CSRF protection

We do NOT use tracking cookies, advertising cookies, or third-party analytics.

8. Children's Privacy

ForensicBridge is not intended for users under 18 years of age. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. We will notify you of material changes via email or through the Service. Your continued use after changes constitutes acceptance.

10. Contact Us
