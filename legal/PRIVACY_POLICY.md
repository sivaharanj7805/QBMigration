## 1. Introduction

ForensicBridge ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our QuickBooks Desktop to QuickBooks Online migration service.

---

## 2. Information We Collect

### 2.1 Information You Provide
- **Account Information:** Name, email address, company name
- **QuickBooks Credentials:** OAuth tokens (not your password) to access QuickBooks Online

### 2.2 Information We Process (Not Store)
- **Financial Data:** Invoices, bills, customers, vendors, accounts, and other QuickBooks data
- **Note:** This data is processed in memory for migration purposes and is NOT permanently stored on our servers

### 2.3 Automatically Collected Information
- **Log Data:** IP address, browser type, access times
- **Usage Data:** Features used, migration statistics

---

## 3. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:
- Perform the migration from QuickBooks Desktop to QuickBooks Online
- Generate audit certificates and verification reports
- Provide customer support
- Improve our services
- Comply with legal obligations

---

## 4. Data Processing and Storage

### 4.1 Zero-Persistence Architecture
Your QuickBooks financial data is:
- Encrypted during transit using AES-256-GCM
- Processed in memory only
- Immediately discarded after migration completion
- Never stored on our servers

### 4.2 What We Do Store
- Your account information (name, email)
- Migration metadata (dates, entity counts, status)
- Audit trail logs (for compliance purposes)

### 4.3 Data Location
All data processing occurs in Canadian data centers (AWS ca-central-1, Montreal).

---

## 5. Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures:
- **Encryption:** AES-256-GCM for data at rest and in transit
- **Hashing:** SHA-256 cryptographic verification
- **Access Control:** Role-based access with multi-factor authentication
- **Compliance:** SOC 2 Type II, HIPAA, and PCI-DSS ready

---

## 6. Third-Party Services
